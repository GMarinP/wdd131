welcome to the bookshelf

This website is made by bookworms for bookworms. We can share our favorite recommendations and look through other reviews to find new books! There is also a random book picker for those of us who can't decide what to read from our TBR. Thank you to all the editors and the users who spend their time sharing what they love most.

do you want to find new books?

We have a page where you can look through reviews made by our editors and other users. You are also able to make your own reviews for the books you love or like a little less.

store your books in our database

We have a page where you can store the titles of books you have read before. Just type the name and press a button and the title is going to be displayed. It is useful to help you keep track of your library.

check out our TBR randomizer

Don't know what to read next? When you add your TBR list to our program, it can randomly select your next read. Just simply type the names of the books you want to read and let the website choose for you.
