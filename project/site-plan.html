site name

I chose this name because it is easy to remember and easily explains its purpose.

site purpose

This site provides a places for bookworms to easily store data from the books they've read, but also to find new books to read.

scenarios

  • What should I read next?
  • How many books have I read?
  • What are some good fanstasy books?

color schema

The color schema is based in shades of brown and green to bring feelings of comfort, friendship, relaxation and new begginings.

  • Primary color: #937666 Used as background color for header and footer.
  • Secondary color: #341C1C Used for titles and texts.
  • Accent color 1: #F6E4F6 Used as background color for the main body and accents.
  • Accent color 2: #7CAE7A Used as a color for when links are hovered and accents.
  • Accent color 3: #232E21 Used as background color for accents.

typography

The font Inconsolata is seen in the titles and the font Montserrat is seen in the texts.
